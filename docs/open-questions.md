## Out of scope, but worth knowing about

**Myndighedsbetjening (authority/government service work) is a different obligation category, not
modeled by this calculator.** It covers strategic authority work — not research, not teaching. Two things distinguish it from
frikøb-for-research: (1) if the work is a "strategisk ydelse" rather than a "kerneydelse" and
can't feed back into the person's own research, Vipomatic registers it **1:1** — no 60/40 split at
all, unlike ordinary frikøb; (2) VAT/moms treatment for this kind of contract depends on whether
AU has a **monopol** (sole possible supplier, no competition) on the specific task — monopol means
momsfritaget, otherwise it depends on the contract's DR1/DR2/DR4 classification and how much of it
is genuinely forskningsbaseret. `Projektenheden` (a central AU unit) makes that determination case
by case. Neither of these change anything about the calculator's actual scope (research buy-out),
but if a future request describes hours registered 1:1 without the usual teaching-share reduction,
this is very likely why — don't treat it as a bug in the frikøb model.
